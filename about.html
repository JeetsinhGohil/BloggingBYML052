About Campus Notes

A peer-to-peer knowledge sharing platform built for college students.

Our Purpose

Campus Notes was created to solve a common problem among college students: the isolation of knowledge. Every semester, students create brilliant assignments, compile excellent study notes, and build insightful projects, only for them to sit forgotten on a local hard drive once the semester ends.

We believe that knowledge grows when shared. Campus Notes provides a centralized, easy-to-use platform where students can publish their academic work, helping their peers learn, and creating a lasting repository of knowledge for future batches.

Key Features

  • Publish Assignments: Easily format and share your assignments and study notes.
  • Discover Content: Search and filter through a rich database of student-generated content.
  • Manage Your Portfolio: Keep track of your contributions through a dedicated dashboard.
  • Save Drafts: Work on your posts incrementally and publish when ready.
